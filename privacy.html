Privacy policy

Effective October 1, 2026. Homebridge Personal is used by its owner for personal home automation.

Data use and storage

The integration uses Google authorization credentials, selected Nest device identifiers and status, device events, and camera video or snapshots to expose authorized devices in Apple Home. Credentials, configuration, logs, and cached snapshots are stored on the owner's Homebridge machine. Video and events are processed by Google services and the local integration; Apple Home devices and services receive data needed for enabled Home features, including recordings if the owner enables them.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. It is used to provide the personal smart-home functionality described here, not to train generalized AI models. Access and retention depend on the owner's Google, Homebridge, and Apple Home settings.

Owner control

The owner can revoke authorization through Google Account connections, disable the integration, and delete local credentials, logs, or snapshots. Recordings retained by Apple Home are managed through Apple Home settings.

This website

This static site does not receive camera feeds or Google authorization credentials and has no application forms, trackers, or analytics scripts. Its hosting provider may process ordinary web request data under its own privacy policy.

For questions about this personal integration, use the developer contact shown on its Google consent screen.
